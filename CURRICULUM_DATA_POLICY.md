# Curriculum data policy

The content catalogue must follow the revised Nigerian curriculum, not generic subject lists.

## Primary 1-3

The approved NERDC offering is English Studies, Mathematics, one Nigerian Language, Basic Science, Physical & Health Education, CRS/IS, Nigerian History, Social and Citizenship Studies, Cultural & Creative Arts, and optional Arabic. **Government is not a Primary 1-3 subject.**

## Primary 4-6

Use English Studies, Mathematics, one Nigerian Language, Basic Science and Technology, Physical & Health Education, Basic Digital Literacy/Digital Technologies, CRS/IS, Nigerian History, Social and Citizenship Studies, Cultural & Creative Arts, Pre-vocational Studies, plus optional French/Arabic.

## JSS1-3

Use the revised JSS offering: English Studies, Mathematics, one Nigerian Language, Intermediate Science, Physical & Health Education, Digital Technologies, CRS/IS, Nigerian History, Social and Citizenship Studies, Cultural & Creative Arts, one Trade subject, Business Studies, plus optional French/Arabic.

## SSS1-3

Use the revised Senior Secondary offering. Core subjects are English Language, General Mathematics, one Trade subject, Citizenship and Heritage Studies, and Digital Technologies. Science, Humanities, Business and Trade subjects should be exposed according to the student's selected field/combination.

## Data rules

1. Never put Government into Primary 1-6.
2. Never put Biology, Chemistry or Physics into Primary 1-3 as standalone secondary subjects; Primary 1-3 uses Basic Science.
3. Do not copy a lesson into an unrelated class merely because the subject name matches.
4. A lesson must resolve through `topic -> class + subject + term -> course -> section`.
5. Syllabus-generated questions must retain their class/term metadata from their tags when the source provides it.
6. Prefer the syllabus documents stored in Supabase Storage as the source for detailed weekly topics. External web sources may be used for validation and gap-filling, not to override the stored official curriculum.
